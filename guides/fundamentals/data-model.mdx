## Sync frequency

Brandstack refreshes most data in near real time. Here is what that means in practice:

- **Shopify orders and refunds**: typically within a few minutes of the event
- **Ad spend and impressions**: updated at least hourly, sometimes faster
- **Marketplace orders and payouts**: pulled daily or as soon as the marketplace report is available
- **GA4 sessions**: updated daily for the previous day
- **Logistics tracking**: updated as carriers push new events, usually every few hours

<Note>
  The first time you connect a source, Brandstack may take up to 24 hours to fully backfill your historical data. After that, new data flows in automatically.
</Note>
