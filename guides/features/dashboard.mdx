The Brandstack Dashboard is your single-screen command center. It pulls live data from Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Instagram, marketplaces, and logistics partners so you can see what is happening across your business without opening ten tabs.

\[Screenshot: Dashboard overview showing live P&L, sales chart, ad spend tiles, recent orders, and top movers\]

## What it shows

Your dashboard is organized into live tiles that update throughout the day:

- **Live P&L:** Revenue minus ad spend and direct costs, updated in near real time.
- **Sales snapshot:** Total orders, average order value, and revenue trend for today, this week, or any custom range.
- **Ad spend summary:** Combined spend across Meta Ads and Google Ads with a quick comparison to your revenue.
- **Orders feed:** Recent orders with status, channel, and value so you can spot unusual patterns fast.
- **Top movers:** Products that are gaining or losing velocity compared to the previous period.

The layout is designed for speed. At a glance you know whether today is on track, where spend is spiking, and which products need attention.

## Why it matters

As a D2C founder, switching between Shopify, ad managers, and courier portals burns time and creates blind spots. You might see strong Shopify sales and miss that Meta spend has already blown through the daily budget. Or you might notice a sales dip without realizing it is tied to a stockout on a top SKU.

The dashboard removes those gaps by unifying every signal into one view. Instead of reactive firefighting, you get proactive awareness. That matters because small delays in spotting a problem, like a campaign overspend or a fulfillment bottleneck, can cost thousands by the end of the week.

<Note>
  The dashboard refreshes data based on each platform's API limits. Most Shopify and ad data updates within minutes, while some marketplace and logistics feeds may lag slightly.
</Note>

## What you can do with it

- **Start every day with clarity:** Open the dashboard first and know your numbers before you check email.
- **Catch anomalies early:** A sudden drop in contribution margin or a spike in ad spend triggers an instant investigation.
- **Prioritize your time:** If top movers show a new product trending, you can double down on creatives or inventory before competitors catch on.
- **Share the view internally:** Show your team the same numbers so everyone is aligned on daily targets and performance.
