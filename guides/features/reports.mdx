The Brandstack Reports module lets you turn live dashboard data into shareable, scheduled reports. Whether you need a weekly founder digest, a client update for your agency, or a custom CSV export for your CFO, you can build and automate it without touching a spreadsheet.

\[Screenshot: Reports builder showing scheduled report list, custom slice editor, and sharing options\]

## What it shows

- **Scheduled reports:** Automated PDF and CSV deliveries sent daily, weekly, or monthly to any email list.
- **Weekly founder digest:** A curated summary of P&L, top movers, ad spend, and highlights designed to be read in under two minutes.
- **Agency client reports:** White-labeled or branded reports that agencies can send to clients summarizing performance across channels.
- **Custom slices:** Build reports from any combination of metrics and dimensions, such as SKU profitability by channel, or ad spend by campaign over a custom date range.
- **Delivery log:** Confirmation that reports were generated and delivered, with retry status if a delivery fails.

## Why it matters

Founders and marketers waste hours every week copying numbers into slides and spreadsheets for investors, partners, or internal reviews. The process is error-prone, out of date the moment it is sent, and distracting from the actual work of growing the brand.

Brandstack Reports automates that grunt work. Your reports pull live data, stay current, and arrive in inboxes automatically. That means your team spends less time formatting and more time acting on what the numbers say. Agencies improve client retention by delivering polished, consistent updates without manual effort.

## What you can do with it

- **Automate your weekly standup:** Set a founder digest to land every Monday morning with the prior week's highlights.
- **Impress clients and investors:** Send clean, data-rich PDF reports that update themselves instead of static slide decks.
- **Slice data on demand:** Export a custom CSV of SKU-level contribution margin for a specific time period to share with your operations team.
- **Build once, send forever:** Create a standard agency report template and apply it to every new client with one click.

<Tip>
  Use the custom slice builder to save frequently requested report configurations. Once saved, anyone on your team can regenerate the same report without rebuilding it.
</Tip>
