The Brandstack Finance module turns raw transaction data into a clear picture of where your money is actually going. It is built for founders, marketers, and agencies who need to move beyond headline revenue and understand true profitability at the brand and SKU level.

\[Screenshot: Finance overview showing gross-to-net waterfall chart, contribution margin trend, and SKU profitability table\]

## What it shows

- **Contribution margin:** Revenue minus variable costs including product cost, shipping, payment gateway fees, and ad spend. This tells you how much each sale contributes to covering fixed costs and profit.
- **Gross-to-net waterfall:** A visual breakdown that starts with gross revenue and steps down through discounts, refunds, gateway fees, shipping, and ad costs to arrive at net profit.
- **Cash flow view:** Inflows from sales and marketplace payouts minus outflows for ad spend and logistics, so you can see liquidity trends without building spreadsheets.
- **SKU profitability:** Per-product profit after all direct costs. Identify which SKUs are your real earners and which are silently losing money.
- **Refund and RTO drag:** Total cost of returns and return-to-origin shipments, including lost shipping fees and reverse logistics, so you can measure the real damage.

### Key terms

- **Contribution margin:** The profit left from a sale after subtracting all variable costs tied to that sale.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total ad spend divided by new customers acquired in a period.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Predicted total revenue a customer will generate over their relationship with your brand.

## Why it matters

Most D2C brands track revenue daily but review true profitability monthly, if at all. By the time you realize a SKU is unprofitable, you have already bought more inventory and run more ads for it. Refunds and RTOs, especially in cash-on-delivery markets, can quietly erode 10 to 20 percent of gross revenue.

Finance in Brandstack closes that lag. You see contribution margin daily, spot drag from refunds or RTOs immediately, and trace profit leaks to specific SKUs or channels. That speed matters when you are deciding whether to restock, kill a product, or renegotiate with a logistics partner.

## What you can do with it

- **Kill losers faster:** Use SKU profitability to discontinue or reformulate products that look good on revenue but destroy margin.
- **Negotiate with data:** Show carriers or marketplace partners exactly what returns and delays are costing you.
- **Model cash needs:** Use the cash flow view to time inventory purchases and ad budget increases without guessing.
- **Align marketing and finance:** Share contribution margin trends with your media buyer so campaigns are optimized for profit, not just sales.

<Warning>
  Contribution margin excludes fixed costs like salaries and rent. Use it to optimize daily operations, not as a replacement for full financial statements.
</Warning>
