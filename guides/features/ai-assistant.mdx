Brandstack's AI Assistant lets you skip dashboards and spreadsheets. Type or ask questions in plain English, and the assistant queries your connected data sources, then returns a chart plus a concise, natural-language answer you can act on immediately.

\[Screenshot: AI Assistant chat interface showing a question and a generated chart with a summary answer\]

## What it shows

The AI Assistant renders results as a combination of:

- **Visual charts** pulled straight from your live Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, or GA4 data
- **Short written answers** that summarize trends, comparisons, or single-number lookups
- **Suggested follow-ups** so you can dig deeper without retyping queries

The assistant understands D2C metrics out of the box. If you are new to a term, here is a quick reference:

- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Revenue generated for every dollar spent on ads. A ROAS of 4 means you earned 4 dollars for every 1 dollar in ad spend.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** The average cost to acquire one new customer, typically including ad spend and attributed costs.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** The total revenue a customer is expected to generate over the course of their relationship with your brand.

## Why it matters

Founders and marketers lose hours every week pulling numbers from six different tabs. The AI Assistant removes that friction by letting you ask exactly what you need, when you need it. No SQL, no pivot tables, and no waiting for a weekly report.

<Accordion title="Example questions you can ask">
  - What was my ROAS on Meta last week?
  - How did my CAC change month over month?
  - Which product had the highest LTV this quarter?
  - Show me revenue by channel for the last 30 days
</Accordion>

## What you can do with it

- Run ad-hoc analysis during campaign reviews without opening multiple platforms
- Share quick answers in team Slack threads by copying the chart and summary
- Spot trends fast by asking comparative questions like "compare Google vs Meta ROAS"
- Build your own muscle memory for key numbers by asking the same question weekly
