The Brandstack Marketing module gives you a single place to understand how every advertising dollar is performing. It connects Meta Ads, Google Ads, and GA4 data so you can move beyond platform-native reports and see the full picture of spend, return, and creative effectiveness.

\[Screenshot: Marketing overview showing unified spend chart, ROAS comparison tiles, creative leaderboard, and cohort payback timeline\]

## What it shows

- **Unified spend:** Total ad investment across all connected platforms, viewable by day, week, or campaign.
- **Blended ROAS:** Overall return on ad spend calculated as total revenue divided by total ad spend across all channels.
- **Channel ROAS:** Individual ROAS for Meta Ads and Google Ads so you can compare performance side by side.
- **Creative leaderboard:** Ranked view of your top-performing ads and creatives by spend, revenue, and ROAS, so you know what messaging and visuals are actually working.
- **Cohort payback:** The number of days it takes for a customer cohort to generate enough revenue to cover its acquisition cost, also known as CAC payback period.

### Key terms

- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Revenue generated for every unit of currency spent on advertising. A ROAS of 4 means you earn 4 for every 1 spent.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total ad spend divided by the number of new customers acquired in the same period.
- **Blended ROAS:** Total revenue from all sources divided by total ad spend, including branded and organic sales.
- **Channel ROAS:** Revenue attributed to a specific ad platform divided by spend on that platform.

## Why it matters

Running Meta Ads and Google Ads in silos leads to inflated confidence. A platform report might show strong ROAS because it ignores organic or repeat purchases. Or a creative that looks great on Meta might underperform when you factor in returns and fulfillment costs.

Brandstack Marketing solves this by anchoring every metric to real revenue and customer behavior. You see blended ROAS alongside channel ROAS, spot when one platform is riding the coattails of another, and identify which creative concepts scale and which flame out after the first week.

<Tip>
  If your blended ROAS is healthy but channel ROAS is weak on one platform, that channel may be overclaiming attribution. Shift budget toward the underreported channel and retest.
</Tip>

## What you can do with it

- **Reallocate budget with confidence:** Shift spend toward channels and creatives with the highest true ROAS, not just the highest platform-reported metric.
- **Scale winners faster:** Use the creative leaderboard to double down on top ads before they saturate.
- **Fix payback timelines:** If a cohort payback stretches beyond 60 to 90 days, tighten targeting or raise average order value to recover CAC faster.
- **Align agency and internal teams:** Show your media buyer the same blended and channel numbers so there is no debate about which platform deserves more budget.
