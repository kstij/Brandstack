Brandstack's white-label mode lets agencies and multi-brand operators present a polished, branded analytics experience to clients or internal stakeholders. Swap the Brandstack logo for your own, host the dashboard on a custom domain, and apply your brand color palette so the platform feels like a natural extension of your service.

\[Screenshot: White-label settings panel showing logo upload, custom domain input, and color picker preview\]

## What it shows

Once white-label is enabled, viewers see:

- **Your logo** in the top navigation and on shared reports
- **A custom domain** such as analytics.youragency.com instead of the default Brandstack URL
- **Your primary and accent colors** applied to charts, buttons, and highlights throughout the dashboard

## Why it matters

Agencies win trust by looking cohesive. When a client opens their performance dashboard and sees your logo and domain, the experience feels proprietary, not borrowed. Multi-brand teams also benefit: each brand can have its own identity inside the same underlying data infrastructure.

<Tip>
  White-label is typically available on agency and enterprise plans. Contact your Brandstack account manager to confirm access and custom domain requirements.
</Tip>

## What you can do with it

- Deliver client report links that reinforce your agency brand on every view
- Onboard new brands under a single account, each with a distinct visual identity
- Remove third-party branding from stakeholder reviews and investor updates
- Maintain one data backbone across your portfolio while the surface stays personalized per brand
