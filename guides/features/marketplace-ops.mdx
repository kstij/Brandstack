The Brandstack Marketplaces module brings your Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra operations into one place. Instead of logging into three seller portals to check orders, track returns, reconcile payouts, and monitor inventory, you get a single view that updates automatically.

\[Screenshot: Marketplaces overview showing order volume by platform, return rate chart, payout status, and stock health alerts\]

## What it shows

- **Orders across platforms:** Unified order feed with status, value, and SKU breakdown for Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
- **Returns and refunds:** Return volume and reasons by marketplace so you can spot product or packaging issues fast.
- **Payout reconciliation:** Expected versus received payouts with fee breakdowns, including commissions, shipping fees, and marketplace charges.
- **Stock health:** Inventory levels by marketplace warehouse with low-stock and out-of-stock alerts.
- **Performance metrics:** Key seller health indicators such as order defect rate, late shipment rate, and customer feedback summary.

## Why it matters

Selling on multiple marketplaces in India means juggling different fee structures, return policies, and inventory requirements. A stockout on Myntra during a promotion, or a spike in Flipkart returns from a single SKU, can cost you listing rank and revenue before you notice it in the native portal.

Brandstack Marketplaces gives you cross-platform visibility without the manual work. You catch stock risks before they trigger penalties, dispute payout discrepancies with proof, and trace return spikes to their root cause before they damage your seller rating.

## What you can do with it

- **Prevent stockouts:** Use stock health alerts to replenish inventory at the right marketplace warehouse before listings go down.
- **Dispute unfair charges:** Compare expected and actual payouts by marketplace to identify overcharged commissions or incorrect returns.
- **Improve product quality:** If returns spike for a single SKU across Flipkart, inspect that batch for defects, packaging, or sizing issues.
- **Optimize marketplace mix:** Compare net profitability per marketplace after fees and returns to decide where to push inventory and promotions.

<Note>
  Marketplace data sync frequency depends on each platform's API availability. Amazon data typically refreshes faster than Flipkart and Myntra.
</Note>
