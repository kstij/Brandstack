Brandstack watches your key metrics around the clock and notifies you the moment something shifts outside the normal pattern. Instead of discovering a broken campaign or a logistics problem in your weekly review, you get a concise alert with context, usually within minutes of the anomaly forming.

\[Screenshot: Alerts panel showing a recent CAC spike alert with context and a link to the detailed metric view\]

## What it shows

Each alert includes:

- **The metric that moved:** Such as ROAS, CAC, delivery SLA, or refund rate
- **Expected baseline:** The typical range Brandstack calculated from your historical data
- **Actual value and timestamp:** When the anomaly started and how far it deviated
- **Quick-link to the full metric view:** So you can inspect related data immediately

## Why it matters

A ROAS drop or a refund spike can cost thousands if it runs for a full day undetected. Anomaly alerts turn reactive reporting into proactive damage control. You catch problems early, act fast, and protect margins without staring at dashboards all day.

<Warning>
  Anomalies reflect unusual data patterns, not guaranteed business problems. Always verify the context in the linked metric view before making major decisions.
</Warning>

## What you can do with it

- Route alerts to a dedicated Slack channel so your marketing or ops lead sees them instantly
- Set email alerts for your morning inbox review if you prefer async triage
- Click through from any alert straight into the affected metric to investigate root causes
- Adjust alert sensitivity over time so you get fewer false positives as your business learns its own rhythm
