Brandstack lets you invite your entire team and external agencies into one workspace so everyone sees the same data. This page walks you through sending invites, choosing the right role for each person, and understanding how Agency Guest access works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Team**.

    \[Screenshot: Settings menu with Team highlighted\]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an email address">
    In the **Invite a team member** field, type the email address of the person you want to add. You can invite multiple people at once by separating emails with commas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Open the **Role** dropdown and choose the access level for this person:

    | Role | What they can do |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Owner** | Full access to billing, integrations, team management, and data. |
    | **Admin** | Can add or remove users, manage integrations, and view all reports. |
    | **Analyst** | Can build dashboards, run reports, and export data. Cannot manage users or billing. |
    | **Viewer** | Read-only access to dashboards and reports you share with them. |
    | **Agency Guest** | Access limited to specific brands you select. Ideal for external marketing or creative agencies. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send invite**. The person will receive an email with a link to join your workspace. If they do not accept within 7 days, the link expires and you can resend it from the Team page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Agency Guests only see the brands you explicitly assign to them during invitation. They cannot browse other brands, view billing, or manage team members.
</Note>

## What happens next

Once a team member accepts the invite they appear in the Team list with their role. You can change roles or remove access at any time from **Settings \> Team**.
