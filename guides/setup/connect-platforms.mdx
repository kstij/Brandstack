Brandstack pulls data from your storefronts and ad platforms into one place. We recommend starting with your primary e-commerce platform, such as Shopify, and then connecting the ad channels that drive traffic to it. This page walks you through the connection process step by step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Integrations page">
    From the left sidebar, click **Integrations**, then choose **Add new source**.

    \[Screenshot: Integrations page with “Add new source” button highlighted\]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your platform">
    Select the platform you want to connect first. We suggest starting with your main storefront, then adding ad platforms afterward.

    Recommended order:

    1. Shopify (or your primary storefront)
    2. Meta Ads
    3. Google Ads
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect and authorize">
    Click **Connect**. You will be redirected to the platform's OAuth sign-in page. Log in with the account that has admin access, review the permissions, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose accounts">
    After authorization, select the specific accounts, stores, or ad accounts you want Brandstack to access. You can add more later from the same integration page.

    \[Screenshot: Account selection screen with checkboxes for specific ad accounts\]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the initial backfill">
    Brandstack will start an initial backfill to import historical data. This usually completes within a few minutes, although large ad accounts may take up to an hour. You will see a status indicator on the Integrations page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Connecting both Meta Ads and Google Ads unlocks blended ROAS in your dashboard, so you can see true return on ad spend across channels in one number.
</Tip>

## What happens next

Once the backfill finishes, your data appears in the Brandstack dashboard. Repeat the steps above for each additional platform you want to connect.

For platform-specific details, visit [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview).
